Counsellors

Independent practitioners who work with people leaving high-control groups, including former Jehovah's Witnesses. They are not Open Space Support staff. If one of them feels like a fit, you can request to be put in touch.

Listing someone here is signposting, not a guarantee that they are the right counsellor for you. Open Space Support does not take bookings, fees, or clinical responsibility for their work.

This is not a crisis service. If you need immediate support in the UK, call Samaritans free on 116 123. For practical help rather than counselling, you can apply for support.

Nadja Zollinger

Heilpraktikerin (Psychotherapie) · Germany

In person or Zoom English and German Trauma-informed

As a Heilpraktikerin (Psychotherapie), an alternative practitioner for psychotherapy based in Germany, I support people through challenging life phases, working in both English and German.

In my long-term therapy practice, I combine clinically grounded psycho- and trauma therapy, using methods such as Somatic Experiencing® (SE), NARM®, and humanistic psychotherapy, with a dharma-informed approach to inner development and growth. This combination draws on both established psychotherapeutic modalities and years of lived experience in India and time spent at a Zen monastery, a background that roots my work in various contemplative traditions, including Sufism, Zen, and Tibetan Buddhism. I offer a space where psychological and physical awareness meet spiritual inquiry, supporting clients amid bodily, emotional, and existential strain with compassion and depth. My therapeutic stance is shaped by a fundamental trust in the human capacity for self-healing, paired with clear, structured guidance in demanding processes.

Qualifications include

Somatic Experiencing, NARM, humanistic psychotherapy training, mindfulness training, and grief support.

Hannah Speirs

Psychotherapeutic counsellor · Merthyr Tydfil

Online and in person Faith deconstruction Identity and autonomy

I am a psychotherapeutic counsellor offering a compassionate space for individuals navigating the experience of leaving, questioning, or recovering from a high-control religious environment. Stepping away from a belief system or community can involve complex and conflicting emotions, including grief, fear, anger, confusion, guilt, and a profound sense of uncertainty about identity and belonging.

My approach is collaborative and centred on your autonomy. I will not seek to replace one set of beliefs with another, but will support you in exploring your experiences, developing self-trust, and making choices that feel authentic to you. Together, we can gently explore the impact of your past experiences while creating space for healing, growth, and a renewed sense of freedom. I offer both online and in-person sessions from Merthyr Tydfil and surrounding areas.

You do not have to navigate this journey alone.

Areas of counselling specialism

Psychosynthesis psychotherapy, with particular interests in addiction and recovery, acute experiences of mental health distress, faith deconstruction and spiritual questioning, identity, autonomy, life transitions, and the process of developing a deeper and more authentic sense of self.
