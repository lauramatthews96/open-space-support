Counsellors

Independent practitioners who work with people leaving high-control groups, including former Jehovah's Witnesses. They are not Open Space Support staff. If one of them feels like a fit, you can request to be put in touch.

Listing someone here is signposting, not a guarantee that they are the right counsellor for you. Open Space Support does not take bookings, fees, or clinical responsibility for their work.

This is not a crisis service. If you need immediate support in the UK, call Samaritans free on 116 123. For practical help rather than counselling, you can apply for support.

Online and in person Cult recovery Former Jehovah's Witnesses

Nadja Zollinger

Heilpraktikerin für Psychotherapie · Germany

In person or Zoom English and German Trauma-informed

Nadja Zollinger is a Heilpraktikerin für Psychotherapie (alternative practitioner for psychotherapy) based in Germany, working both in English and German. Her long-term therapy practice weaves together dharma-based psycho- and trauma therapy, drawing on years of lived experience in India and time spent at a Zen monastery — a background that grounds her work in various contemplative traditions, including Sufism, Zen, and Tibetan Buddhism. Alongside this, she brings clinical rigour in trauma-informed care, working with methods such as Somatic Experiencing (SE), NARM, and humanistic psychotherapy. She offers a space where psychological healing and spiritual inquiry meet, supporting clients in navigating difficulty with both compassion and depth. Sessions are available in person or via Zoom by arrangement.

Qualifications include

Somatic Experiencing, NARM, humanistic psychotherapy training, mindfulness training, and grief support.

Ask us to put you in touch. We'll pass your request on.

Dr Ryan Lee

Clinical psychologist · Online appointments only

Online only Cult recovery Former Jehovah's Witness

Dr Lee is a former Jehovah's Witness with a doctorate in clinical psychology and seven years of experience specialising in cult recovery. In addition to his clinical practice, he produces the podcast Welcome to the World, focused on the challenges of leaving high-control religious groups, and leads trauma-informed healing retreats in Peru for people recovering from religious trauma.
