2. What we are, and are not

We offer practical information and a way to ask for support if you are rebuilding after leaving a high-control religious group.

We are not:

  • a crisis service, emergency service, or suicide helpline

If you are in crisis or need immediate support in the UK, contact Samaritans on 116 123 (free, 24/7), or call 999 if you or someone else is in immediate danger.

4. Applying for support

Submitting an application does not create a contract for a particular service, a guaranteed outcome, or an ongoing duty of care beyond what we reasonably can offer as volunteers.

We will read what you send and respond using the contact details you give us, usually within a few days, but we cannot promise a particular timescale. You can ask us to delete an application at any time.

You are responsible for making sure the contact details you give us are safe for you to use. If it is not safe for us to call, email, or write at certain times, tell us in the application.

Draft applications are saved in your browser on the device you are using. They are not on our servers until you submit. If that device is shared, a saved draft may be visible to someone else who uses it.

5. Donations

You can make a one-off or monthly donation through our donate page. Donations are voluntary gifts to help keep this work going. They do not buy a service, membership, or a guaranteed outcome, and they do not create a contract for support.

We are not a registered charity, so we cannot claim Gift Aid and your gift is not a charitable donation for tax purposes.

Payments are taken on a secure page run by Stripe. We never see your full card number. Stripe’s own terms apply to the payment itself.

You should be 18 or over to donate. If you set up a monthly gift and want to stop it, use the link in Stripe’s receipt or confirmation email, or contact us and we will cancel it.

Donations are gifts, so we do not usually refund them. If you paid by mistake, or were charged twice, contact us promptly and we will look at it.
