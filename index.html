Dear friend,

If you've found your way here, chances are you already know that leaving isn't the end of the story. It's usually where the harder chapter starts, and that's exactly why we built this.

Every one of us on the Open Space Support team were once Jehovah's Witnesses. We know what it's like to rebuild a career when your qualifications don't exist, or don't count. We know what it's like to sit with a bank account, a CV, or a doctor's form for the first time and realise no one ever showed you how. We know the particular kind of quiet that comes with losing family, friendships, and a community all at once, and we know how long it can take to feel steady again.

We didn't build this from research alone. We built it from having lived it.

That means we're not here to tell you what to believe, or to relitigate what you left behind. We're here for the practical part, the part that so rarely gets talked about, and that nobody else seems to be building for. Work, money, housing, education, family, and the harder emotional weight underneath all of it.

There's no test to pass here, and nothing you need to explain before we'll help. Start wherever makes sense to you today.

With understanding,

The Open Space Support Team

Three others worth knowing about

We didn't build these, and we'd still point you to them. All three are free, and all three were made by people who've left a high-control religion themselves.

Free online course

Flaxenwick

Sixteen short video lessons on healing and rebuilding after leaving the Jehovah's Witnesses, each with a worksheet to work through at your own pace. It starts where most people actually need to start - immediate needs, physical and mental health, building a support network - before moving on to boundaries, purpose, and handling triggers.

Built on life-coaching principles. The course says itself that it isn't a substitute for professional mental health support.

Visit Flaxenwick →

Free downloadable booklet

Olive Leaf Network

"Thinking of leaving?" is a clear, thorough guide for anyone still weighing up whether to go. It was written by former members of a different high-control group, and almost all of it carries straight across. Read it in your browser or download it to keep.

The download deliberately arrives named like a recipe file, so it doesn't stand out on a shared device.

Read "Thinking of leaving?" →

In-person meetups, UK

XJW Friends

A group of ex-Jehovah's Witnesses who have been meeting up since 2007, with socials in London and the Midlands and associated groups in York and Edinburgh. It's faith-neutral, with one rule: no preaching, in either direction.

The group is private and venues are shared with members only. Their Meetup page is the one they keep active.
